Case study 01

Generating 3,364 Leads for a Recruitment Campaign

Category: Lead GenerationPlatform: Meta Ads – Facebook & Instagram
Objective: Generate recruitment enquiries through lead forms.

The Challenge

A voice and video calling platform needed a consistent flow of potential female host applicants. The campaign needed to attract relevant prospects while keeping lead acquisition costs under control.

Our Strategy

We developed and managed a Meta Ads lead generation campaign focused on audience relevance and lead volume. Our approach included:

  • Audience research and targeting.
  • Custom-designed ad creatives and copy.
  • Instant Lead Forms with qualifying questions.
  • Campaign setup and launch.
  • Retargeting and A/B testing.
  • Ongoing budget and delivery optimization.

The Results

3,364Lead form submissions
$0.03Average cost per lead
$111.31Total ad spend

The Takeaway. A structured lead generation campaign, supported by relevant creatives and optimized targeting, delivered a consistent flow of recruitment enquiries at a low acquisition cost.
