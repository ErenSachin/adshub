Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ADS HUB, powered by SS Enterprises (“we”, “us”), collects and uses information when you visit this website or contact us. By using the website you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what you choose to submit through our forms or messages, plus limited technical data that your browser sends to the website host.

3. Personal Information

Depending on the form you use, this may include:

  • Name, email address, and phone or WhatsApp number
  • Business name, website or social handle, city, and industry
  • Service required, advertising budget range, and campaign goals
  • Your message and any preferred call date and time
  • For job applications: role, experience, platforms worked on, budget handled, and an optional profile link

4. How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, schedule calls, prepare proposals, manage client relationships, evaluate job applications, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Contact Form Information

When you submit a form, the details are sent to us through a webhook into our lead management or CRM system. If that is unavailable, you may be asked to send the same details to us by WhatsApp or email, which are then handled by those providers under their own policies. A time slot chosen on a booking form is a request, and is confirmed by us.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your light or dark theme and currency choice in your browser’s local storage, which stays on your device. If we add analytics or advertising pixels (for example Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel), we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

7. Third-Party Services

We rely on third parties that may process limited data: Google Fonts (your browser requests fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address), our website hosting provider (standard server logs), WhatsApp and your email provider if you message us, and the CRM or automation provider that receives form submissions. Each has its own privacy policy.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us and limit access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We do not claim any security certification.

9. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and application details only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, and for legitimate business and legal record-keeping. After that we delete or anonymize them.

10. User Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us, and to stop contacting you. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions, use the Contact Us page to make a privacy request or ask a question about this policy.
